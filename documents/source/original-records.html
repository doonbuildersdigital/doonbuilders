How to read this transcription

Italic, underlined — entered by hand on the original (the documents are typed forms filled in by hand).
Dotted underline — handwritten; the reading is uncertain.
[illegible] · [torn] — text that cannot be read, or is missing where the paper is damaged.
Wording, spelling, punctuation and layout follow the original. Only letters that the typewriter clearly mis-struck (e.g. “tae” for “the”) are corrected; these are listed in the notes on each page.

Document 1 · Work Order · First Generation18 March 1976
OFFICE OF THE EXECUTIVE ENGINEER,
IRRIGATION DIVISION, DEWAS.
NO. 1397/APR/SAC/ Dewas, dated the 18/3/76 / 1976.
To,
M/s Shri Dilip Singh S/o Pritam Singh Contr.
Radha Ganj, Dewas
Sub:-Const. of 6′ fall at Ch. 11.00 of Minor Canal No. 2 of P.K. Tank project.
Ref:-Your Application dated __________/cost of works Rs. 4925/-/-.
------------
1/Having executed the agreement in form "D" on piece work system by you, the above work is hereby awarded to you to as per rates entered in the above Agreement.
2/The above rates are subject to the relevant notes of relevant chapter of the "Current Schedule of Rates" of Irrigation Upper Chambal Circle, Indore read with the General Instructions of the "Current Schedule of Rates" referred to above.
3/If any item contingent to the execution of the work as per agreement is got executed for which there is no rates in the above schedule of rates payment for the same will be made at the rate mentioned in the current schedule of rates of Upper Chambal Circle, Indore.
4/You are requested to start the above work and completed by One Month.
5/For details of work to be executed the Sub-Divisional Officer, P.K. [illegible] Canal Sub-Dn. Agar may please be contacted. The work is to be executed as per instructions of the above officer under his supervision.
6/The rates for lead provided in the agreement are for maximum lead but payment will be made to the extent of actual lead in value at the proportionate rate.
Encl:-
[signed]
( S. K. VARMA )
EXECUTIVE ENGINEER,
IRRIGATION DIVISION, DEWAS.
ENDT. NO. ______ /SAC/Dewas, dated the ___/___/1976

Copy is forwarded to the Sub-Divisional Officer :- P.K. Canal Sub-Dn. Agar

2/He should please note specially the provision para 6 of the work order while making payment.
3/The Auditor, P.K. Canal for information and necessary action and guidance. A copy of the agreement No. 178/[illegible]-76 pertaining to the work is enclosed for information and [torn]
Notes on the original
  • Typed form; the number, date, addressee, subject, cost, completion period, sub-division and agreement number are filled in by hand in ink.
  • The word “to” in para 1 is struck through on the original. A handwritten marginal note in blue ink appears beside paras 3–4.
  • Typewriter mis-strikes corrected: “tae” → the, “sjstem” → system, “notds” → notes, “Cirble” → Circle, “masimum” → maximum, “won order” → work order.
  • The lower edge of the sheet is torn; the end of the last line of the endorsement is missing.
